Retail HVAC Solution for Style Union

Retail Project

Project overview

A comfortable, footfall-ready store climate for Style Union with concealed ducting and even temperature across the retail floor.

The engagement followed our full HVAC lifecycle, a detailed site assessment and heat-load study, right-sized system selection, clean installation and commissioning, followed by testing, balancing and a structured maintenance handover. The outcome is dependable comfort with efficient, predictable operation.

The brief

Style Union needed a store environment that stays comfortable regardless of how many people are on the floor at any given moment. Retail cooling lives and dies on first impressions, a shopper stepping in from the Bengaluru heat should feel the difference within moments, and that comfort has to hold whether the store is quiet on a weekday morning or packed during a weekend sale. The brief called for fast pull-down cooling, even temperature across fitting rooms, display aisles and the billing counter, and a system discreet enough to stay out of the store's visual merchandising.

The challenge

Retail floors present a genuinely variable heat load, body heat from footfall, heat gain from display lighting and glass frontage, and door traffic that lets warm outside air in every time a customer enters. Fitting rooms, generally enclosed and further from the main air path, tend to run warmer than the open floor if distribution isn't planned carefully. The store's ceiling and shopfitting layout also limited where ductwork and diffusers could sit, since nothing could interrupt sightlines to merchandise or signage. Working within an active retail lease also meant the installation window had to be tight, coordinated closely with the store's fit-out and opening timeline.

Our engineered solution

We designed a concealed ducted AC system sized to handle rapid pull-down after door-traffic loads, with diffuser placement planned around the store layout so distribution stays even from the entrance right through to the fitting rooms and billing area. Duct runs and grilles were positioned to blend into the ceiling and shopfitting, keeping the cooling invisible against the retail fit-out. Capacity was calculated against the store's actual footfall and lighting profile rather than a generic retail benchmark, so the system neither struggles at peak hours nor overshoots during quieter periods.

Ducting integrated into the shopfit

Duct runs and grilles were routed to sit inside the store's shopfitting rather than compete with it, so distribution stays even without a single visible pipe run breaking the retail fit-out.

Execution & commissioning

Installation was planned around the store's fit-out schedule to avoid delaying opening, with ducting and diffusers fitted ahead of ceiling and display work so the final finish stayed clean. Coordinating closely with the electrical and shopfitting contractors kept the programme moving without clashes over ceiling space or cable routes. Post-installation, we tested and balanced airflow across every zone of the floor, checked fitting-room temperatures against the open aisles, and confirmed pull-down performance under simulated door-traffic conditions before handover.

The outcome

The store now cools quickly and holds an even temperature across the floor no matter how busy it gets, with fitting rooms and the billing counter as comfortable as the main aisles. The ducted system stays out of sight, keeping the visual merchandising intact, while a structured AMC keeps performance consistent through Bengaluru's warmer months and high-footfall seasons alike. For Style Union, that translates directly into shopper experience, customers spend longer on the floor when the store feels comfortable from the moment they walk in, without ever noticing the system working behind the scenes.

Outcome. Shoppers feel the difference within moments of walking in, and the cooling stays even whether the floor is quiet or packed for a weekend sale.

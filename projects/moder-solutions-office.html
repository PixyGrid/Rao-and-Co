Smart Office HVAC for Moder Solutions

Corporate Project

Project overview

A smart-controlled VRF office system for Moder Solutions delivering zone-wise comfort and measurable energy savings.

The engagement followed our full HVAC lifecycle, a detailed site assessment and heat-load study, right-sized system selection, clean installation and commissioning, followed by testing, balancing and a structured maintenance handover. The outcome is dependable comfort with efficient, predictable operation.

The brief

Moder Solutions wanted an office climate system that matched how a modern workplace actually operates, cabins, open-plan desks, meeting rooms and a server area all with different occupancy patterns and cooling needs, run from a single intelligent system rather than a collection of standalone units. The ask went beyond comfort: the facilities team wanted visibility and control, the ability to schedule cooling around working hours, tune individual zones without touching the whole floor, and keep energy use in check without manual intervention every day.

The challenge

A working office has uneven, shifting loads through the day, a packed meeting room in the morning, a quiet cabin in the afternoon, a server room that needs round-the-clock cooling regardless of business hours. Retrofitting smart zoning into an occupied office also meant planning installation around live workstations and minimising disruption to daily operations. Getting the controls right mattered as much as getting the equipment right: the system needed to be simple enough for non-technical staff to adjust, while still giving facilities managers granular, zone-level oversight. There was also a practical constraint on installation timing, cabling and indoor units had to go in without pulling the team off their desks for an extended stretch.

Retrofitted without disrupting the floor

Piping, cabling and indoor units were routed zone by zone around live workstations, so staff kept working through most of the installation instead of losing their desks for an extended stretch.

Our engineered solution

We specified a VRF system architecture that lets each zone, cabins, the open floor, meeting rooms and the server area, run to its own setpoint and schedule from shared outdoor condensing capacity, avoiding the inefficiency of independent standalone units. Smart controls were layered on top for scheduling, zone-wise temperature management and centralised monitoring, so the facilities team could set working-hours profiles, override individual zones on demand, and keep the server room on continuous, independent cooling. Equipment selection favoured energy-efficient VRF technology suited to the office's variable load profile.

Keeping the server room on its own independent path was a deliberate design choice, it meant routine scheduling changes on the office floor would never risk affecting equipment that needed to run uninterrupted.

Execution & commissioning

Installation was phased to avoid disrupting ongoing work, with indoor units, refrigerant piping and controls wiring routed to keep the office presentable throughout. Once live, we configured the smart control platform alongside the Moder Solutions facilities team, set up zone schedules and access levels, and ran full commissioning checks, verifying each zone held its setpoint independently, confirming the server room's dedicated cooling path, and balancing airflow across the floor. Staff were walked through the controls before handover.

The outcome

The office now runs on a single coordinated system that adapts to how the space is actually used, cooling scales down in unoccupied cabins, ramps up in busy meeting rooms, and the server area stays protected independent of the rest of the floor. With scheduling handling routine operation and an AMC covering upkeep, the facilities team spends far less time manually managing comfort, and energy use tracks more closely with real occupancy rather than running flat out all day. It has also given Moder Solutions a system that can grow with the office, since new zones or schedule profiles can be added to the same VRF backbone without a fresh round of equipment planning.

Outcome. Cooling now scales with real occupancy, quieter cabins draw less, busy meeting rooms draw more, and the server room stays protected around the clock.

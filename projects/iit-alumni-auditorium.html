Auditorium HVAC for IIT Alumni Facility

Institutional Project

Project overview

High-occupancy auditorium cooling with balanced air distribution and low acoustic footprint for a premium alumni facility.

The engagement followed our full HVAC lifecycle, a detailed site assessment and heat-load study, right-sized system selection, clean installation and commissioning, followed by testing, balancing and a structured maintenance handover. The outcome is dependable comfort with efficient, predictable operation.

The brief

The IIT Alumni facility's auditorium needed to cool a large, high-occupancy volume evenly, quickly and quietly, comfortable for a packed hall within minutes of filling up, without the noise or draught that large-space systems can introduce. As a flagship space used for lectures, gatherings and alumni events, the brief placed equal weight on acoustic performance and thermal comfort: any mechanical noise or uneven cooling would have been immediately noticeable in a space designed for focused listening. The facility also wanted a system that could stand up to the intermittent, high-intensity way an auditorium is actually used, rather than a layout designed around steady, predictable occupancy. Alongside comfort, the brief also touched on efficiency, since the hall would sit idle for long stretches between events.

The challenge

Auditoriums present a distinct load profile, occupancy, and therefore heat load, swings sharply between an empty hall and a full house, and that swing can happen quickly as events start and end. The volume and ceiling height made air distribution difficult to get right; conventional layouts risk stratification, with cool air pooling near the floor while the upper volume stays warm, or draughts on the seats nearest the outlets. Keeping noise levels low enough for lectures and presentations added a further constraint on equipment selection and placement. The auditorium's height and open volume also meant standard duct runs and diffuser spacing needed to be reworked to suit the scale of the room, with careful attention paid to how air would move across a fully occupied seating bowl.

Our engineered solution

We designed the system around large-space air distribution principles, throw patterns and diffuser placement calculated to spread conditioned air evenly across the seating bowl without creating localized cold spots or draughts. Equipment was selected and positioned with acoustic performance as a primary criterion, keeping mechanical noise low enough not to interfere with speech or presentations. The system was also designed to respond efficiently to the auditorium's variable occupancy, ramping cooling up ahead of events and stepping back during low-occupancy periods rather than running a flat, energy-heavy load around the clock. This gave the facility team a system that matches the auditorium's actual usage pattern instead of a one-size setpoint held constant regardless of occupancy.

Planned around a live events calendar

Every stage of design and installation was checked against the auditorium's booking schedule, so lectures, gatherings and alumni events already on the calendar were never at risk of a clash with our work.

Execution & commissioning

Installation was coordinated around the auditorium's event calendar to avoid clashing with scheduled functions, with ducting and diffuser work carried out during quieter windows. Airflow was tested and balanced across multiple points in the seating bowl to confirm even distribution from the front rows to the back, and acoustic checks were carried out with the system running to verify noise levels stayed within a range suitable for lectures and events. The facility team was walked through the controls so cooling could be scheduled around the auditorium's own event calendar.

The outcome

The auditorium now cools evenly across its full seating capacity, holding comfortable conditions through the swing from an empty hall to a full house without noticeable draughts or noise. Events and lectures run without the mechanical system becoming a distraction, and the alumni facility has a large-space cooling setup built specifically for how the auditorium is actually used, backed by ongoing maintenance support that keeps the system ready for the venue's next event. For a space that hosts everything from academic lectures to large alumni gatherings, that consistency has become part of what makes the auditorium dependable to book and use.

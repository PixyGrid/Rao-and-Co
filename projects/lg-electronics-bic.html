Experience Centre HVAC for LG Electronics BIC

Commercial Project

Project overview

A quiet, evenly-conditioned experience centre for LG's Brand Innovation Centre with discreet ducting and rapid pull-down cooling.

The engagement followed our full HVAC lifecycle, a detailed site assessment and heat-load study, right-sized system selection, clean installation and commissioning, followed by testing, balancing and a structured maintenance handover. The outcome is dependable comfort with efficient, predictable operation.

The brief

LG Electronics approached us to condition a customer-facing Brand Innovation Centre where the HVAC itself had to disappear from the experience. The brief was straightforward to state and demanding to execute: even, immediate comfort across open display zones and smaller demo rooms, near-silent operation so product audio and presenter voices carry cleanly, and no visible ductwork or hardware that could compete with the exhibits. Cooling needed to feel effortless to visitors moving between brightly lit display bays and enclosed rooms, without any perceptible temperature step between zones.

Purpose-built cassette units for demo rooms

Each enclosed demo and meeting room needed its own temperature control independent of the main floor, so cassette units were specified room by room rather than shared off a single zone.

The challenge

Experience centres carry heat loads that a typical office does not, dense display lighting, running screens and demonstration units all add continuous internal heat gain, on top of glazing exposure common to showroom frontages. The interior fit-out further limited our options: a shallow false-ceiling plenum in the main display floor constrained duct runs, while the enclosed demo rooms needed independent temperature control without borrowing capacity from the main floor. Grille and diffuser placement had to be agreed jointly with the branding and interior teams so that nothing broke the visual language of the space. Adding to the complexity, the centre needed to remain operational for staff training and soft previews even as parts of the installation were still underway, which meant staging the work zone by zone rather than shutting the whole floor down at once.

Our engineered solution

We designed a hybrid layout: concealed ducted AC for the open display floor, giving broad, even air distribution through low-profile diffusers set flush with the ceiling, paired with cassette units for the enclosed demo and meeting rooms where independent zoning mattered more than uniform throw. Each zone was sized against its own heat-load calculation rather than a blanket average, so display bays with heavier lighting loads received proportionately more capacity. Ductwork routes and diffuser positions were planned around the display layout first, keeping every visible element discreet.

This split-system approach meant the main floor stayed visually uninterrupted while the smaller rooms retained the flexibility to run at different temperatures for private demonstrations or client meetings.

Execution & commissioning

Installation was sequenced closely with the interior fit-out crew to keep ducting, cabling and diffuser cut-outs aligned with the display build, minimising rework and protecting the finished ceiling lines. Once systems were live, we carried out air balancing across every zone, checked for draft-free throw near display units, and verified sound levels stayed low enough not to interfere with product demonstrations or ambient audio. Controls were configured and handed over with a clear operating routine for the facilities team.

The outcome

The Brand Innovation Centre now runs on a climate system that visitors never have to think about, consistent temperatures from the entrance through to the demo rooms, quiet enough to keep focus on the products, and dependable through long open hours. With commissioning documented and an AMC in place, LG's facilities team has a predictable, low-friction system to rely on as the centre continues to host visitors and showcase new launches. The zoned layout also gives them room to reconfigure demo areas for future launches without having to revisit the core cooling design each time.

Outcome. A climate system visitors never notice, even comfort from the entrance to the demo rooms, quiet enough to keep focus on the products.

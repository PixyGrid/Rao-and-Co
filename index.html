About Rao & Co

A name trusted for reliable air conditioning

With years of hands-on experience delivering HVAC and air conditioning solutions across Bengaluru, Rao & Co has earned the trust of homeowners, businesses and industries by creating spaces that stay comfortable through the city's warm, humid summers and everything in between.

From heat-load calculation and system planning to installation, commissioning and long-term maintenance, we handle the complete HVAC lifecycle, delivering cooling that performs reliably, runs efficiently and adds lasting value to every space we serve.

Sustainable HVAC

Cooling that's kinder to energy bills & the planet

Energy is the largest lifetime cost of any HVAC system. We engineer for efficiency from day one, right-sizing equipment, specifying inverter and heat-recovery technology, and tuning controls so you cool only what you need, when you need it.

Right-Sized, Inverter-Driven

Correct load calculation plus inverter compressors cut energy waste and hold a steady temperature.

Heat & Energy Recovery

Energy-recovery ventilation reclaims cooling from exhaust air to lower fresh-air loads.

Smart Controls & Zoning

Scheduling, zoning and BMS integration eliminate the cooling of empty spaces.

Low-GWP Refrigerants

Modern R-32 and R-410A systems deployed and serviced to reduce environmental impact.

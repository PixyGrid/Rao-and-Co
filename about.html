Who We Are

Comfort, engineered for every space

Rao & Co is a Bengaluru-based HVAC and air conditioning company delivering dependable cooling for commercial, industrial and residential environments. What began as a focused service team has grown into a full-lifecycle HVAC partner trusted across the city.

We combine engineering-led design with hands-on execution, sizing systems correctly, installing them cleanly, and supporting them for the long run. Clients stay with us because we prioritise the right, most efficient solution over the easy sale.

Based in Jayanagar, Bengaluru Operating since 2015 Energy-efficient focus
Our Story

From a focused service team to a full-lifecycle HVAC partner

Rao & Co began in 2015 as a small, hands-on air conditioning service team in Bengaluru. As clients asked us to take on more, design, installation and long-term maintenance, we grew into a company that handles the complete HVAC lifecycle under one roof.

Today we work across homes, offices, retail, healthcare and industry, but the approach has not changed: assess the space properly, size the system correctly, install it cleanly, and stand behind it for years.

Sustainable HVAC

Cooling that's kinder to energy bills & the planet

Energy is the largest lifetime cost of any HVAC system. We engineer for efficiency from day one, right-sizing equipment, specifying inverter and heat-recovery technology, and tuning controls so you cool only what you need, when you need it.

Right-Sized, Inverter-Driven Systems

Correct load calculation plus inverter compressors cut energy waste and steady the temperature.

Heat & Energy Recovery

Energy-recovery ventilation reclaims cooling from exhaust air to lower fresh-air loads.

Smart Controls & Zoning

Scheduling, zoning and BMS integration eliminate cooling of empty spaces.

Low-GWP Refrigerants

Modern R-32 and R-410A systems deployed and serviced to reduce environmental impact.

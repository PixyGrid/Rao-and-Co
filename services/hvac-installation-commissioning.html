Installation done right the first time

Rushed, untidy installation is the root cause of most early HVAC problems, refrigerant leaks, poor airflow, noise and premature compressor failure. Our technicians follow manufacturer specifications and proven site practices for every stage: equipment erection, refrigerant and chilled-water piping, ducting, drainage, electrical and controls.

We install split, cassette, ducted, VRF/VRV and centralized systems across residential, commercial and industrial projects in Bengaluru, coordinating with your civil, electrical and interior teams to keep the site on schedule.

Commissioning that proves performance

Installation is only complete once the system is commissioned. We test, balance and verify every system against its design intent so you receive comfort you can measure, not just equipment that switches on.

We treat your site with respect, organised material handling, tidy routing and clear communication throughout. For fit-outs and new builds, we work to your programme so HVAC is never the reason a project slips.

Our installation process, step by step

Execution follows a fixed sequence rather than being improvised on site. We start with a pre-installation survey to confirm the approved drawings still match ground conditions, mark out equipment locations and routing, and stage material before any cutting or drilling begins. Outdoor units are positioned with service access and vibration isolation in mind; indoor units are erected level and secured to the structure, not just the false ceiling grid.

Refrigerant piping is cut, brazed under nitrogen purge and pressure-tested before insulation goes on, a step that is easy to skip and expensive to regret later, since a slow leak behind finished insulation can take months to show up as poor cooling.

Piping, ducting and workmanship that holds up

Most HVAC problems that surface within the first year trace back to workmanship, not equipment. We size and insulate ducts to the design's static-pressure calculations, seal joints properly to control air leakage, and slope condensate drainage correctly so water never backs up into the space. Piping supports are spaced to manufacturer standard rather than "whatever the ceiling allows," which prevents the sagging and vibration noise that plague rushed installations.

Testing, balancing and commissioning before handover

Once erection is complete, we move into testing, adjusting and balancing (TAB), measuring actual airflow and refrigerant parameters at every outlet and adjusting dampers and settings until the system matches the design intent, zone by zone. This is what turns a correctly installed system into one that actually delivers even, predictable comfort rather than hot and cold spots across the same floor.

Commissioning also includes verifying electrical safety, checking controls and thermostat programming, and confirming refrigerant charge against manufacturer tables rather than by feel.

Safety on site and documentation at handover

Elevated work, refrigerant handling and live electrical connections all carry risk, and our crews follow standard safety practice throughout, proper access equipment, lockout procedures during electrical work, and PPE as a baseline rather than an afterthought. At handover, you receive commissioning reports, warranty registration, as-built drawings and operating instructions, so the system is fully documented from the day it starts running rather than becoming a black box the first time it needs service.

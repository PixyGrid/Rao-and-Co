What a structured AMC actually does

Regular preventive maintenance is the cheapest insurance your HVAC system will ever have. Scheduled visits catch small issues before they become failures, keep efficiency high and extend equipment life, often by years. Systems under a good AMC break down far less and run noticeably cheaper.

Preventive or comprehensive, your choice

Preventive AMC covers scheduled servicing and inspections, keeping your system healthy at a predictable annual cost. Comprehensive AMC additionally covers parts and repairs for fully budgeted peace of mind. We help you choose based on the age, criticality and usage of your equipment.

Built for Bengaluru businesses and homes

From single-home units to multi-system commercial installations, our AMC plans are structured around your actual usage. For businesses, that means fewer disruptions and lower lifetime cost. For homes, it means reliable comfort without nasty surprises when you need cooling most.

Inside a typical AMC visit

Our technicians follow a structured checklist on every visit rather than a quick look-and-leave. That consistency is what separates a real maintenance programme from an occasional service call, and it is what keeps performance predictable through the year.

Filter Inspection

Cleaning or replacement of air filters.

Coil Cleaning

Evaporator and condenser coils cleared.

Drain Line Clearing

Prevents condensate water leakage.

Refrigerant Check

Pressure and gas-level verification.

Electrical Checks

Connection tightening and capacitor testing.

Control Calibration

Thermostat and control fine-tuning.

The exact checklist is tailored to what the system actually is, a single split unit in a home needs a shorter visit than a bank of ducted units serving an office floor, and cassette or VRF systems bring their own indoor-unit and outdoor-unit checks. We keep a record of what was done at each visit, so the history of every unit is visible over time, not just the most recent service.

Why Bengaluru’s climate makes AMC worthwhile

Bengaluru’s dust load and long monsoon months put particular strain on outdoor units and filters, dust cakes coils faster than in drier cities, and heavy rain can push debris into drain lines and outdoor units. A structured AMC schedule accounts for this, timing coil cleaning and drain checks around the seasons rather than waiting for a complaint call.

Key takeaway. Dirty coils and off-spec refrigerant charge make compressors work harder, quietly raising electricity bills long before a breakdown ever shows up.

How AMC protects your investment

A compressor works harder, and wears out sooner, when coils are dirty and refrigerant charge is off. Left unchecked, this shows up as rising electricity bills long before it shows up as a breakdown. Routine AMC visits catch that drift early, keeping running costs in check and adding years to the practical life of the equipment.

We often see units that have never had a professional service beyond the occasional filter wash, and by the time they fail outright, the compressor or PCB is usually the casualty, which is by far the most expensive repair on any system. A scheduled AMC catches the smaller warning signs first, well before they escalate into a costly repair bill.

What to expect when you sign up

We start every AMC with a short site survey to log each unit, its age and condition, then agree a visit calendar suited to usage, more frequent for kitchens or dusty environments, standard for offices and homes. Each visit closes with a written report, so you always know exactly what was checked and what, if anything, needs attention.

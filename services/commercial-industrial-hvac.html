Efficiency at the scale where it counts

At commercial scale, small efficiency gains translate into large savings. We right-size plant, specify efficient equipment, and integrate smart controls, heat recovery and zoning so you are not paying to cool empty floors. The lifetime saving typically dwarfs any difference in upfront cost.

Reliability your operations can depend on

Downtime in a commercial or industrial setting is costly. We commission thoroughly, document clearly, and back the installation with structured maintenance and priority support, so your HVAC quietly does its job while you run your business.

Plant rooms and large-scale ductwork

At commercial and industrial scale, the plant room is the heart of the system, chillers, pumps, air handling units and cooling towers all need to be laid out for serviceability, not just performance on day one. We design plant rooms and ductwork runs with maintenance access, noise control and future capacity in mind, so the system stays workable as the facility grows.

Ductwork sizing and routing matter just as much as the plant itself, undersized risers or poorly balanced branches lead to some floors running cold and others running warm, regardless of how well the chiller performs. We model duct runs and static pressure at design stage so airflow reaches every zone as intended, not just the zones nearest the plant room.

Key takeaway. Integrating chillers, AHUs and VRF systems into a single BMS lets a facilities team monitor and adjust every zone from one dashboard, instead of walking floors to check equipment.

BMS integration for centralized control

Large facilities benefit from visibility across every zone, not isolated units running on their own schedules. We integrate chillers, AHUs and VRF systems with building management systems so operations teams can monitor performance, schedule run times and spot anomalies from a single dashboard, rather than walking floors to check equipment.

Beyond monitoring, integration also means the system can respond automatically, staging chillers to match actual load and adjusting VRF zones to occupancy. For facilities running extended hours, that kind of automated oversight reduces both energy waste and the burden on an in-house maintenance team.

Common challenges we solve for Bengaluru facilities

Many commercial and industrial projects in Bengaluru involve retrofitting HVAC into buildings that were not designed for it, tight plant room space, limited duct routing and phased occupancy that means the system must be commissioned in stages. We plan around these constraints early, rather than discovering them mid-installation.

The cooling and ventilation challenges of production environments

Factories and workshops rarely behave like standard commercial spaces. Machinery, motors, compressors and process lines throw off heat loads that shift through the day depending on what is running, and Bengaluru's warm, humid climate adds to that base load rather than offsetting it.

Add high-bay volumes, large open floor plates, dust, fumes and moisture from washing or finishing processes, and the air handling problem becomes very different from cooling an office or a home. Standard split systems, sized on rule-of-thumb tonnage, are usually the wrong tool, they short-cycle, struggle with heat concentrated near equipment, and do little for the fumes and airborne particulate that accumulate around machining, welding or packaging lines.

Engineered around real heat sources

Getting this right calls for an engineering read of the shop floor, not a generic quote. We look at where the heat actually originates, a bank of CNC machines, a compressor room, a furnace or oven, and design cooling and extraction around those sources rather than treating the whole building as one uniform zone.

How Rao & Co approaches industrial HVAC

We start with a proper load calculation that accounts for machinery heat gain, occupancy, roof and wall construction, and process-specific factors like ovens, dryers or compressed air systems, in addition to the outdoor design conditions typical of Bengaluru. From there we design a system, often a mix of process cooling, general ventilation and targeted extraction, that keeps critical zones within tolerance without over-conditioning areas that don't need it. Where product quality or equipment reliability depends on a tighter temperature or humidity band, we design to that specification rather than a general comfort standard.

Systems and equipment we typically deploy

Industrial floors call for robust, serviceable equipment rather than delicate, decorative units. Depending on the facility, this can include VRF and ducted systems for office and control-room areas, packaged and chilled-water plant for larger process cooling loads, high-volume low-speed or exhaust fans for general air movement, and dedicated fume and dust extraction at source. We select equipment that can run long duty cycles reliably and that your maintenance team can actually service without specialised tools or long shutdowns.

For larger, continuous process cooling loads, packaged and chilled-water plant is often the more dependable choice over a bank of light-commercial split units.

Uptime, energy cost and compliance considerations

In a production facility, an HVAC failure is rarely just a comfort issue, it can mean a stopped line, spoiled batches or unsafe working conditions from heat and fume build-up. We design with redundancy and serviceability in mind where the process demands it, and we size equipment so it isn't running flat out just to keep pace, which shortens component life and inflates energy bills. Energy cost matters more at industrial scale than almost anywhere else, so system selection and control strategy are chosen with running cost, not just installation price, in view. We also keep ventilation rates and extraction aligned with applicable workplace safety norms so air quality on the floor stays within acceptable limits.

Why manufacturers choose Rao & Co, and how we work with you

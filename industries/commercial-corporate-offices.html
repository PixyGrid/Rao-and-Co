The comfort challenge inside a Bengaluru office

Bengaluru's climate is mild for much of the year, but office floors rarely feel that way. Dense workstation layouts, rows of monitors, large glazed facades and back-to-back meeting rooms all add heat load that a single thermostat setting cannot manage. A cabin facing west can run several degrees warmer than an internal server room, while a meeting room packed with ten people and a projector heats up within minutes of the door closing. A single-zone air conditioning setup struggles here, it either overcools the open floor to keep meeting rooms comfortable, or leaves hotspots that draw complaints.

We approach office HVAC as a zoning problem first and an equipment problem second. Before recommending any system, we walk the floor plate, note where teams sit, where meeting rooms cluster, where server racks or pantries generate extra heat, and how occupancy shifts through the day. That mapping shapes the design far more than a tonnage calculation on its own.

Systems we typically deploy for offices

Most Bengaluru offices, from single-floor startups to multi-tenant business parks, are well served by VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) systems, which let us run one outdoor condensing unit against many indoor units, each with independent temperature control. For smaller offices or standalone cabins, cassette or ducted split units offer a simpler route to the same zoned comfort.

Fresh air, IAQ and after-hours scheduling

Offices are closed environments for eight to ten hours a day, and indoor air quality has a direct bearing on alertness through the afternoon. We size fresh-air intake and exhaust separately from the cooling load, so ventilation is never compromised to hit a tonnage number. Where floors run high occupancy, trading floors, call centres, dense co-working spaces, we pay particular attention to CO2 build-up and design air changes accordingly.

Scheduling matters just as much as the hardware. We programme zone-wise schedules around actual working hours, meeting room bookings and weekend skeleton staff, so the system isn't cooling an empty floor on a Friday evening, this is often the single largest lever on an office's energy bill.

Why corporate teams choose Rao & Co

Facility and admin teams don't want air conditioning to be a recurring conversation. They want a system quiet enough not to interrupt calls, even enough that no one argues over the thermostat, and reliable enough that a breakdown doesn't become an all-hands problem. Our design-first approach, proper load calculation, correct zoning and commissioning that's actually tested, not just switched on, is what keeps offices out of that cycle. We also schedule retrofits and upgrades around live occupancy, working floor-by-floor or after hours so teams keep working while we install.

From site inspection to ongoing AMC

Every office engagement starts with a site walk-through and load assessment, followed by a proposal that spells out zoning, equipment and expected running costs, not just a quote. Once installed, we test and balance airflow room by room before handover. After that, our AMC plans cover filter cleaning, refrigerant checks and seasonal servicing, with priority response for breakdowns, so comfort stays predictable through Bengaluru's warmer months and the busiest quarters on the calendar.

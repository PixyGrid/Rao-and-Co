Plan Your Home's Cooling

Why home cooling in Bengaluru needs more thought than it gets

Bengaluru's climate is milder than most Indian cities, which is exactly why home cooling here is so often done badly. Many households and builders default to a single tonnage figure per room without accounting for orientation, glazing, floor level or how a space is actually used, and the result is either an undersized unit that runs constantly and never quite cools, or an oversized one that short-cycles, leaves the room clammy, and costs more to buy and run than it should.

In apartments and villas alike, every room has a different heat load, a west-facing living room with large windows behaves nothing like an interior bedroom, and treating them the same is where most home AC installations go wrong from day one.

Getting it right the first time

Getting this right at the outset saves years of frustration. It means fewer callbacks, quieter operation, and a running cost that actually reflects efficient equipment rather than a unit fighting a load it was never sized for.

How we approach residential HVAC, room by room

We size each room individually rather than applying a blanket formula across a home or a development. That means factoring in window area and orientation, ceiling height, occupancy and even furnishings, so a bedroom gets a quiet, right-sized unit while a sun-facing living room gets the capacity it actually needs. For apartment developments and villa projects, we work with architects and builders early enough to plan concealed piping, condenser placement and electrical provisioning before finishes go in, so residents get a clean installation rather than visible conduit and retrofitted holes.

Systems we recommend for homes and apartments

Inverter split and multi-split systems are the default for most bedrooms and living areas, they modulate output to match the actual load, which keeps noise down and running cost sensible over a full season rather than just on the hottest days. For larger villas or duplexes, ducted and VRF options can condition multiple rooms from a more discreet indoor unit, keeping visible equipment to a minimum. We select from Daikin, LG and Mitsubishi ranges based on the room's noise sensitivity, budget and the aesthetic the homeowner or developer is after.

Whichever system suits the home, the finish is planned as carefully as the equipment itself, piping routed and condensers positioned so nothing looks like an afterthought once the interiors are complete.

Comfort, aesthetics and running cost that residents actually notice

For a home, comfort is judged less by numbers and more by whether a bedroom is quiet enough to sleep in, whether the living room cools quickly after guests leave, and whether the electricity bill still feels reasonable in peak months. We pay attention to indoor unit placement and cable routing so the finished look stays clean, and we favour inverter technology precisely because it holds temperature steadily rather than cycling hard on and off, which is both more comfortable and more efficient over a billing cycle.

Whether it's a single villa or a multi-tower residential development, our process stays the same, a proper site assessment, a clear itemised proposal with no overselling, clean installation and commissioning, and AMC support afterwards so residents aren't left to fend for themselves after the warranty period.

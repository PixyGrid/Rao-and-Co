Why Partnerships Matter

A dealership is a promise, not a logo on the wall

Being an authorized dealer for the industry's leading manufacturers isn't about badges. It is what protects you after the sale. It means the equipment we hand over is genuine, the warranty is valid, spare parts are real, and our engineers are trained on the systems they install. When something needs attention five years from now, that chain still holds.

We partner with Daikin, LG and Mitsubishi Electric because between them they cover almost every requirement in Bengaluru, from a single home split to a multi-zone VRF plant, and because each has earned a reputation for lasting the distance.

Our Manufacturers

Three names we're trusted to represent

Authorized dealer partnerships with the brands that set the standard for reliability, efficiency and after-sales support.

Authorized Dealer · Since 1924

Daikin

The world's number-one air conditioning company and the inventor of VRV technology. Daikin's breadth is unmatched, from residential hi-wall splits, cassettes and ductable units to VRV systems for whole buildings, packaged rooftops and industrial chillers. We supply and install across the full Daikin range, and its reliability makes it a first choice for projects that have to run for years without drama.

LG

LG brings serious engineering to commercial HVAC through its Multi V VRF platform, alongside single-split cassettes, concealed-duct units and a deep chiller line-up covering centrifugal, screw, scroll and absorption. Its inverter compressors and smart controls are built for efficiency and quiet operation, which makes LG a strong fit for offices, retail and mixed-use developments where running cost and noise both matter.
